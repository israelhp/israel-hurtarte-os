# Verify design

Render the prototype (isolated headless Chrome, throwaway profile) and the app at 1440x900, 1024x768, 768x1024 and 390x844, light and dark. Compare tokens, spacing, typography, states. Report DESIGN_FIDELITY_GATE as PASS/FAIL/NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW with screenshots and listed deviations (each justified or fixed).
